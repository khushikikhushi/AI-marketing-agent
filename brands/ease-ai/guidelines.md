EASE AI — WORKING BRAND NOTES (DRAFT)
Derived from the approved "Gap" and workshop campaigns. Replace with an official guide when one exists.

WHO WE TALK TO
Students, educators, professionals, entrepreneurs, creators — everyone, no matter their background. Many already use AI for quick answers; EASE AI shows them what they could build, automate and create.

CORE IDEA
The gap isn't between those who have AI and those who don't — it's between those who know what to do with it and those who don't. Every post should make the viewer feel that gap, then offer the workshop as the next step.

LOOK
- Dark cinematic posters (Night #080C14 / Ember Black #140D0A) or warm light editorial posters (Cream #F4EFE7), with Signal Orange #FF6A13 as the only accent.
- Photography graded to amber and deep shadow: lone figures, screens, city lights, glowing data; or believable UI (chat windows, browser cards, phone apps, agent checklists, workflow chips) shown as proof.
- Big ALL-CAPS grotesk headlines (Inter Tight 800–900, tight tracking), the turn of the line in orange. JetBrains Mono for small tracked labels. Instrument Serif italic for a soft lead-in line.
- Consistent frame: logo tile top-left; FREE pill + LIVE AI WORKSHOP top-right; info band at the bottom with date and multi-city times; orange REGISTER NOW pill.

VOICE
Direct, curious, a little provocative, always inclusive. Questions that make people reflect ("Are you just using AI — or do you know how to unlock it?"). Empowering, never fear-based ("Empowering you — not replacing anyone").

DON'TS
No neon cyberpunk clichés, no robot stock images, no glowing brains, no blue-purple "AI" gradients, no invented dates, venues or numbers.
