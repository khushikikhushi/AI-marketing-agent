01. About This Document
This document defines the visual and verbal identity standards for the Global Excellence Challenge (GEC) brand, including the Clocket practice app and all associated programmes.
Consistent use of these guidelines ensures that GEC is recognised as a premium, world-class academic competition brand across all touchpoints — from school communications and digital platforms to event materials and merchandise.
All partners, schools, designers, and vendors producing materials on behalf of GEC must adhere to these standards.
02. Brand Overview
Vision
To be the world's most respected platform for academic excellence, nurturing analytical and cognitive skills in students globally.
Mission
To inspire and challenge young minds through rigorous, technology-powered competitions that celebrate speed, accuracy, and critical thinking.
Brand Personality
- Prestigious & Authoritative — commanding respect in academic circles.
- Energetic & Inspiring — motivating students to push their limits.
- Precise & Trustworthy — standards-driven with zero compromise on quality.
- Global Yet Personal — international pedigree with local community relevance.
03. Logo Usage
Primary Logo — Global Excellence Challenge (GEC)
The GEC logo features the iconic globe and open book motif, symbolising global reach and academic achievement. The clock embedded in the 'E' represents Clocket — the timed competition format at the heart of GEC programmes.

Secondary Logo — Clocket App
The Clocket logo represents the practice and competition platform used across all GEC programmes. The gold stopwatch on a royal blue face reinforces the brand's core colours and the timed-challenge format.

Clear Space Rule
Always maintain a minimum clear space around both logos equal to the height of the 'G' letterform in the GEC logotype. Never crowd the logo with other graphic elements, text, or edges.
Minimum Size
 Application Minimum Width
 Print materials 25mm
 Digital / Screen 80px
 Favicon / App icon 32px
Logo Don'ts
- Do not stretch or distort the logo.
- Do not recolour the logo outside approved colour variants.
- Do not place the logo on a busy or low-contrast background.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects.
- Do not rotate the logo.
- Do not recreate or substitute the logo with any other font or mark.

Colour Usage Principles
- Royal Navy is the dominant brand colour. Use it for headers, backgrounds, and primary call-to-action elements.
- Championship Gold is the accent colour. Use it for highlights, dividers, icons, borders, and decorative emphasis. Never use gold as a primary text colour at small sizes.
- White is the primary content background. Avoid off-white or cream alternatives unless specifically approved.
- Charcoal (#2C2C2C) is preferred over pure black (#000000) for all body text.
- Never introduce additional colours not specified in this palette without brand approval.
05. Typography
Typography communicates the tone and professionalism of the brand. GEC uses a clean, authoritative type system based on widely available typefaces to ensure consistency across digital and print.
 Style Font Size Use
 Display / Title Calibri Bold 36–44pt Cover titles, event banners
 Section Heading (H1) Calibri Bold 22–26pt Document section titles
 Sub-heading (H2) Calibri SemiBold 16–18pt Sub-sections, card labels
 Body Text Calibri Regular 11–12pt Paragraphs, descriptions
 Caption / Label Calibri Regular 9–10pt Image captions, footnotes
 Table Header Calibri Bold 11pt Table column headers
 Data / Values Calibri Regular 11pt Table cell content
Web & Screen Typography
For digital contexts (websites, apps, presentations), use the following web-safe stack:
Primary: 'Calibri', 'Trebuchet MS', Arial, sans-serif
Monospace / Data: 'Courier New', Courier, monospace
Line Spacing & Paragraph Rules
- Body text: 1.4–1.6× line height.
- Headings: 1.1–1.2× line height.
- Paragraph spacing: 6–8pt after each paragraph.
- Never use fully justified text in digital materials — use left-aligned for readability.
- Titles on dark backgrounds: use white or gold text only.

07. Brand Applications
Print Materials
- School Proposal / Pitch Deck: Royal Navy header, Championship Gold accents, white content body. GEC logo top-left, Clocket logo bottom-right.
- Certificates: Navy border, gold foil-effect typography, GEC logo centred at top.
- Banners & Standees: Full-bleed navy background, gold headline text, logo centred or top-left.
- Letterhead: GEC logo top-left, navy rule below header, contact details in Calibri 10pt.
Digital & Screen
- Social Media (Instagram/Facebook): 1:1 square. Navy background, gold headline, white body text. Always include GEC or Clocket logo.
- Email Headers: 600px wide, navy background, gold rule at base. Calibri 14pt body text.
- WhatsApp/Broadcast Graphics: Keep composition simple — one key message, one logo, max two colours.
- Presentation Decks: Use approved GEC PowerPoint template. Title slides: Navy full-bleed. Content slides: White with navy header bar.
Competition Collateral
- Question Papers: GEC logo top-right, age group and round clearly stated in navy. No decorative elements that could distract participants.
- Scorecards & Tally Sheets: Clean tabular format, navy column headers, alternating pale navy rows.
- Trophies & Medals: GEC logo engraved. Colour: Navy base with gold lettering.
- ID Cards / Lanyards: Navy background, white name text, gold border line.
08. Dos & Don'ts
✔ DO — ✘ DON'T 
- Use official logo files provided by GEC. — - Do not alter logo colours, proportions, or spacing. 
 
- Maintain clear space around logos at all times. — - Do not use the logos on backgrounds that reduce legibility. 
 
- Use Calibri as the primary typeface. — - Do not introduce new fonts without brand approval. 
 
- Use HEX #0D245C and #C9A84C for all brand colour applications. — - Do not use unauthorised colour variations. 
 
- Always use high-resolution assets for print (300 DPI minimum). — - Do not use low-resolution or raster-upscaled logos. 
 
- Refer to approved templates for all competitive materials. — - Do not create competition materials that bypass approved templates. 
 
- Include both GEC and Clocket logos where applicable. — - Do not use the GEC brand in association with content that conflicts with the brand's values.
